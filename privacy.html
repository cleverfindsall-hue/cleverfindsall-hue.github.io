4. The Clever Finds publishing app

The publishing app is an internal tool. It is used only by us, to manage our own accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from other users of those platforms.

What it accesses

  • Basic information about our own connected accounts (account ID, name, profile picture).
  • Permission to upload videos and images, with titles and captions, that we created and approved.
  • Statistics about our own posts (for example views, likes, comment and share counts) to measure which content works. It does not read the content of comments or messages.

How it stores and shares data

  • Access tokens and statistics are stored only on our own computer. They are never sold, shared or transferred to anyone else, and never used for advertising.
  • Data is sent only to the platform it belongs to (for example a video is uploaded to YouTube through YouTube's own API).
  • We keep statistics as long as we need them to compare our content. When access to an account is revoked or the account is disconnected, we delete its access tokens and the data received from that platform within 7 days.

YouTube API Services

The publishing app uses YouTube API Services. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy, and use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. You can revoke the app's access to a Google account at any time on the Google security settings page. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Meta (Facebook, Instagram), TikTok and Pinterest

These platforms process data under their own policies: Meta Privacy Policy, TikTok Privacy Policy, Pinterest Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time in each platform's settings for connected apps (for Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites).
